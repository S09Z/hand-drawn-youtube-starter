# Research — DRY-RUN-001 The Bellweather Umbrella Bell

## Question

Why did the fictional town of Bellweather ring a bell whenever residents opened umbrellas indoors?

## Audience promise

The viewer will understand how an invented safety rule became a ritual after its original purpose disappeared.

## Hook candidates

- This town fined umbrellas—but only indoors.
- A warning bell outlived the danger it was built for.

## Timeline / causal chain

1. 1884: Covered market opens with polished floors.
2. Wet umbrellas create puddles.
3. Staff introduce a warning bell and umbrella stands.
4. Entrance mats solve the hazard, but the bell survives as ceremony.

## Counterpoints and common misconceptions
