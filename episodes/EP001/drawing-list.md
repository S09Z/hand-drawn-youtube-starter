## Reuse and complexity decisions

- Total proposed new assets: 15; no optional decorative asset is approved.
- Character expression and arm changes are layers within the base asset, not separate drawings.
- The bag comparison set supplies all bag states across the episode.
- The machine cutaway supplies the silhouette, working-model view, process animation, patent explanation, and 1879 morph.
- Factory and Patent Office backgrounds should use shallow depth and removable props so they can be repurposed.
- Motion lines, arrows, checkmarks, date cards, and text labels are CapCut/Procreate overlays, not standalone asset records.

## Production limits

- Target new assets: 15 or fewer unless the story clearly requires more.
- Combine similar shots where clarity is not harmed.
- Prefer modular layers and reusable expressions.
- Do not create polished production art until the storyboard and drawing list receive human approval.
