# PHASE-01 — Foundation + Manual Production MVP

## Objective

Prove that one creator can take a topic from research to a published hand-drawn video with a clear, repeatable, low-complexity workflow.

## Included

- Repository/state foundation
- Channel Bible and visual language
- Production templates
- Reusable asset inventory
- One real Episode 001 content pack
- Manual Procreate and CapCut production
- Human-reviewed publishing checklist
- Initial analytics record and retrospective

## Excluded

- Hermes, Telegram, scheduled tasks, and agent gateways
- Vector databases, embeddings, or complex RAG
- Automatic image, video, voice, or publishing pipelines
- Custom web dashboards

## Recommended loop sequence

1. Foundation review
2. Channel Bible
3. Visual style
4. Template dry run
5. Episode research
6. Script
7. Storyboard and drawing list
8. Manual production
9. Publish and measure
10. Retrospective

## Quality gates

- Research distinguishes facts from uncertainty and includes sources.
- Script follows the channel rules and fits the target length.
- Storyboard maps each beat to a visual.
- Drawing list checks existing assets first and avoids needless complexity.
- Human approves script, final edit, and publishing.
- Metrics and qualitative lessons are recorded after publishing.

## Exit criteria

All Phase 1 deliverables exist, Episode 001 has completed the workflow, the retrospective names the strongest bottleneck, and Phase 2 begins only if an integration directly addresses that bottleneck.
